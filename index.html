Crossify101

An educational journey to grow your vocabulary and broaden your cultural horizons — crosswords that mix picture clues and knowledge questions, in 12 languages. No ads. No internet. No tracking.

Free • 4+ • Works fully offline

Why you'll love it

A quiet, focused word game built to teach as you play.

Pictures & questions

Most clues are knowledge questions and definitions across science, history, geography, nature and sports — woven with image clues: identify a flag, a face, a landmark, a painting or an animal from a real photo.

12 languages

Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish and Turkish — each with 24 themed packs and its own progress and points.

Points & mini-games

Spend points on three tiers of hint when you're stuck. Running low? Win them back in quick mini-games — tic-tac-toe, rock-paper-scissors, dice, three cards, coin flip and higher-or-lower.

Private by design

No network requests, no permissions, no analytics, no ads. Nothing ever leaves your device — your progress lives in a single local file.

Learn while you play

Every answer is a real word, name, place or fact worth knowing. Finish a language and you've met hundreds of figures, flags, landmarks and ideas.

Grows with you

24 packs per language ramp from easy starters to harder vocabulary and tougher trivia. The letter bank shows only the letters you actually need — never noise.
