Frequently asked questions

Short answers about price, languages, devices and privacy.

Is Crossify101 free?

Yes. Crossify101 is free, with no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads. The in-game points are a local game mechanic and have no monetary value.

Does Crossify101 work offline?

Yes, completely. Every puzzle, picture and mini-game is bundled inside the app, so you can play anywhere without an internet connection. The app never makes a network request.

Which languages can I play in?

Twelve: Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish and Turkish. Each language has its own 24 themed packs, its own progress and its own points.

What kind of clues are in the puzzles?

Most clues are knowledge questions and definitions across science, history, geography, nature and sports. They are mixed with picture clues where you identify a flag, a face, a landmark, a painting or an animal from a real photo.

How do hints and points work?

Every language starts you with a 10-point welcome bonus. Revealing a letter costs 1 point, revealing the written clue costs 2 and revealing the whole word costs 3. Running low? Win points back in quick mini-games: tic-tac-toe, rock-paper-scissors, dice, three cards, coin flip and higher-or-lower.

Which devices does Crossify101 run on?

iPhone and iPad (iOS and iPadOS 13 or later) and Macs with Apple silicon, from the App Store, and Android devices from Google Play.

Is Crossify101 suitable for children?

Yes. It is rated 4+, shows no ads and collects no data at all, so it collects no data from children either.

Does Crossify101 collect any data?

No. The app requests no permissions and contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or attribution software. Your progress is stored only on your device, in a single sandboxed file.
