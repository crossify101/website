Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 May 2026

Crossify101 collects no data. Truly nothing. The app makes no network requests, requests no device permissions, and contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or attribution software of any kind.

What we collect

Nothing. Crossify101 does not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal information or usage data. We do not see, receive or have any access to information about you.

No network access

The application has no networking layer on its runtime path — it is technically unable to contact any server. On Android, the app does not even declare the INTERNET permission. Everything you see and play is bundled inside the app and runs entirely offline.

No permissions

Crossify101 requests no device permissions whatsoever: no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no storage, no notifications. You can confirm this in your device settings — the app's permission list is empty.

No tracking or advertising

There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no attribution or marketing trackers, and no crash-reporting services. No advertising identifier is read or used.

Where your progress lives

Your game progress — solved levels, point totals and language preference — is stored only on your device, inside a single sandboxed file that belongs to the app. It never leaves the device. On both iOS and Android, automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of this file are disabled, so your progress is not copied to any cloud service.

Because everything is local, deleting the app removes all of its data. We have no copy of it and cannot recover it for you.

Children's privacy

Crossify101 is rated suitable for all ages and is safe for children. As the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Purchases

Crossify101 is free and contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid content. The in-game "points" are a local game mechanic only and have no monetary value.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@crossify101.com.
